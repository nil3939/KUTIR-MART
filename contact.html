Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about shopping with Kutir Mart

How fast is delivery across Bangladesh?

Within Dhaka Metro, deliveries arrive in 24 hours (with same-day options available for select items). For Chittagong, Sylhet, and other districts nationwide, express courier delivery takes 48 to 72 hours.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Cash on Delivery (COD). All online card transactions are protected with 256-bit SSL encryption.

What is your return & exchange policy?

We provide a 7-day hassle-free replacement or refund guarantee on all non-perishable goods if there are defects or damages during transit.
